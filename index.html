How It Works

Step 1: Launch VirtualKeyboard

Open the app from your Applications folder or menu bar. The keyboard appears on screen, ready for use.

Step 2: Position and Resize

Drag to place it anywhere. Adjust size for comfort with your pointing device.

Step 3: Click to Type

Select keys with mouse, trackball, or head pointer. Modifiers like Shift or Command work intuitively for capitals and shortcuts.
